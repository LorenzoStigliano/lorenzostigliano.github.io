Lorenzo Stigliano

I am a DPhil candidate at the University of Oxford, working at the intersection of machine learning and biomedical science. I am funded by the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Healthcare Data Science.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a BSc in Computer Science and Mathematics at the University of Edinburgh, and an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Imperial College London, where I worked in the Brain and Signal Analysis Lab on knowledge distillation for graph neural networks. I subsequently worked as a data scientist at J.P. Morgan in the Applied AI & ML team.

Research

My research focuses on building machine learning models for biology and drug discovery. I am particularly interested in representation learning: how to learn structured, meaningful representations of biological systems. I aim to build models that respect the organising principles underlying living systems, and are therefore better suited to scientific discovery.

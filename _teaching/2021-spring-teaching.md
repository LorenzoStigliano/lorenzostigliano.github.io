I served as a **Tutor** for [*Foundations of Data Science (INFR08030)*](https://www.drps.ed.ac.uk/20-21/dpt/cxinfr08030.htm), a second-year undergraduate course in the School of Informatics at the University of Edinburgh. The course introduced students to the core concepts, methods, and ways of thinking required for modern data science, integrating theory, practice, and critical reflection.

Responsibilities
======
- Supported students during workshops and practical labs, assisting with both theoretical and programming-based exercises  
- Provided guidance on data analysis mini-projects, focusing on reproducibility, clarity of communication, and critical evaluation of results  
- Facilitated discussion-based sessions on real-world implications of data science and ethical considerations  
- Assisted students in developing strong analytical writing and scientific communication skills  

Course Overview
======
This course brought together **statistical, machine learning, and computational techniques** with practical data analysis workflows and critical thinking.  
Teaching combined lectures, workshops, and labs, with practical components using a **Python-based data science ecosystem**.

Core Technical Topics
======
**1. Data Wrangling and Exploratory Data Analysis**
- Working with tabular data  
- Descriptive statistics and data visualization  
- Linear regression and correlation  
- Clustering techniques  

**2. Supervised Machine Learning**
- Classification methods  
- Linear and logistic regression  
- Generalization and regularization  

**3. Statistical Inference**
- Randomness, simulation, and sampling  
- Confidence intervals and the law of large numbers  
- Randomized studies and hypothesis testing

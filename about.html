lorenzo stigliano

i am a d.phil. candidate at the university of oxford, working at the intersection of machine learning and biomedical science. i am funded by the epsrc centre for doctoral training in healthcare data science.

before oxford, i completed a b.sc. in computer science and mathematics at the university of edinburgh, and an m.sc. in artificial intelligence and machine learning at imperial college london, where i worked in the brain and signal analysis lab on knowledge distillation for graph neural networks. i subsequently worked as a data scientist at j.p. morgan in the applied ai & ml team. a full cv can be found here.

research

my research focuses on building scientifically-grounded models for biological data, spanning foundation models and applications in drug discovery. i am particularly interested in representation learning: how to learn structured, meaningful representations of biological systems. rather than treating biological data as generic sequences to be modelled with off-the-shelf architectures, i aim to build models that respect the structure underlying living systems; making them more interpretable, more data-efficient, and better suited to scientific discovery.

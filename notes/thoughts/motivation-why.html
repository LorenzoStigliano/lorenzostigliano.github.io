why i am writing

27 may 2026

motivated by patrick kidger own website, and in particular, his article "just know stuff"1. he explores his own experiences and a exhaustive list of what is essential to learn during a phd in ml. in fact i came across this before starting my current phd and has always been somewhat of a north star when it comes to direction.

as such i have decided to start writing on a simple, no css, no cookies html website with no ai writing. keeping the website simple is important. on the one hand it is easily maintainable and adjustable since i can change things as i like without the need to tinker with css, having to deal with the nightmare of frontend or hacking academic pages2 to my liking. more importantly the simplicity allows me to focus on the content without having to worry about anything else. creating a space where writing can be as bad or as expressive as i want. thoughts, notes, ideas are primary. everything else is secondary.

and maybe these notes could be of interest to anyone out there (highly doubtful). perhaps you're starting your phd or maybe still an undergraduate either way they could give you an insight into someones time during their phd. i guess i am writing to my past self that was always a bit lost and would explore the internet for any guidance. maybe i can look back on this one day, and have a laugh, who knows?

1 patrick kidger article https://kidger.site/thoughts/just-know-stuff/.
2 academic pages github https://academicpages.github.io/.

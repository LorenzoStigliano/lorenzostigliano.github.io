Why I am writing

27 May 2026

This was motivated by Patrick Kidger’s own website, and in particular his article “Just Know Stuff”1. He explores his own experiences and gives an exhaustive list of what is essential to learn during a PhD in ML. In fact, I came across this before starting my current PhD, and it has always been somewhat of a north star when it comes to direction.

As such, I have decided to start writing on a simple HTML website with no CSS, no cookies, and no AI writing. Keeping the website simple is important. On the one hand, it is easily maintainable and adjustable, since I can change things as I like without the need to tinker with CSS, deal with the nightmare of frontend, or hack Academic Pages2 to my liking. More importantly, the simplicity allows me to focus on the content without having to worry about anything else, creating a space where writing can be as bad or as expressive as I want. Thoughts, notes and ideas are primary; everything else is secondary.

And maybe these notes could be of interest to anyone out there (highly doubtful). Perhaps you are starting your PhD, or maybe you are still an undergraduate. Either way, they could give you an insight into someone’s time during their PhD. I guess I am writing to my past self, who was always a bit lost and would explore the internet for any guidance. Maybe I can look back on this one day and have a laugh. Who knows?

1 Patrick Kidger’s article: https://kidger.site/thoughts/just-know-stuff/
2 Academic Pages on GitHub: https://academicpages.github.io/

My current research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning methodology, model efficiency, and biomedical applications. In particular, I focus on three central research questions:

  • How can we learn rich, general-purpose representations from heterogeneous and multimodal data, enabling robust generalization?
  • How can machine learning methods be applied responsibly to healthcare, enabling earlier detection and understanding of ageing-related diseases?
  • How can we design machine learning models that are computationally efficient and suitable for deployment in resource-constrained or safety-critical settings?

I am a D.Phil. candidate in Applied Machine Learning at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/), funded by the [Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC)](https://www.ukri.org/councils/epsrc/) [Center for Doctoral Training in Healthcare Data Science](https://www.bdi.ox.ac.uk/study/cdt). My research focuses on developing and deploying advanced machine learning methods for real-world healthcare applications. 

Before starting at Oxford, I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Mathematics at the [University of Edinburgh](https://www.ed.ac.uk/), graduating with First Class Honours and ranking first in my cohort. Followed by an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/), graduating with distinction, focusing on graph neural networks. During my studies, I was actively involved in undergraduate teaching and tutoring in machine learning, data science, and foundational computing courses.

I previously worked in industry as a Data Scientist in Applied AI & Machine Learning at [J.P. Morgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/technology/artificial-intelligence), where I designed, implemented, and optimized end-to-end machine learning systems deployed in large-scale production environments. My work spanned time-series modelling, representation learning, and retrieval-augmented generation pipelines, with a strong emphasis on scalability, cost efficiency, and measurable real-world impact. Alongside my industry experience, I have maintained a close connection to academic research. I worked as a Machine Learning Research Assistant at Imperial College London in the [Brain and Signal Analysis Lab](https://basira-lab.com/), under the supervision of Prof. Islem Rekik, where I developed computationally efficient knowledge distillation methods for graph neural networks, enabling fast inference and deployment on resource-constrained devices.
